Set on more than five pristine acres along Long Island's storied North Shore, Swan Landing is a custom waterfront estate of extraordinary scale and craft. Twenty-three thousand square feet of formal and family living open onto Manhasset Bay through walls of glass, while the grounds descend past a heated pool and stone terraces to a private beach and deep-water dock.

Thirteen bedrooms and fourteen baths, a chef's kitchen, a paneled library, and a private primary wing — every room is composed around the light and the water. This is a rare offering on one of the Gold Coast's most coveted lanes.
